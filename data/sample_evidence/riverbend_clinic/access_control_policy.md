# Riverbend Family Clinic — Information Access Control Policy

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Riverbend Family Clinic is an invented organization
> used to demonstrate ComplianceGuard AI. This is not a real policy.

Policy owner: IT Manager. Approved by: Practice Administrator. Version 2.1.

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Riverbend Family Clinic controls access to systems that
create, receive, maintain or transmit electronic protected health information
(ePHI), including the electronic health record (EHR), the billing system and
shared network drives.

## 2. Role-based access

Access to ePHI is granted using role-based access (RBAC). Each workforce member
receives only the minimum necessary access based on their job role. Front-desk
staff can view scheduling and demographics; clinical staff can view and update
the clinical record; billing staff can view claims data. Administrator accounts
are limited to IT staff.

## 3. Requesting and approving access

All access requests are submitted through the IT help desk ticketing system. The
workforce member's manager must approve access before IT provisions user access.
The approval and the access granted are recorded in the ticket.

## 4. Access reviews and termination

Managers complete a quarterly access review of every user in their department and
confirm that each user's access still matches their role. When a workforce member
leaves the clinic, IT disables their accounts and revokes all access within 24
hours of the termination notice.

## 5. User identification

Every workforce member is assigned a unique user ID before they can access any
system containing ePHI. Shared or generic accounts are prohibited for the EHR and
the billing system. The EHR audit log records the unique user ID for every record
viewed or changed, so that activity can be traced to an individual user.

## 6. Workstation and session security

Clinic workstations lock automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity and require
the user's password to unlock. The EHR application enforces a session timeout of
15 minutes of inactivity, after which the user must sign in again.
