# Riverbend Family Clinic — IT Operations Handbook (excerpt)

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT created to demonstrate ComplianceGuard AI.

## Help desk hours

The IT help desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 18:00. Outside these hours,
an on-call IT administrator can be reached through the answering service.

## Emergency access procedure

If the EHR is unavailable to clinicians during an emergency (for example a power
failure, a network outage or a ransomware incident), the on-call IT administrator
is the only person authorized to enable the emergency access account. The
credentials for the emergency access account are kept in a sealed envelope in the
Practice Administrator's office safe.

## Backups

Nightly backups of the EHR database are copied to the clinic's network storage and
weekly to an external drive that the Practice Administrator takes off-site.

## Patching

Workstations receive operating system updates on the second Tuesday of each month.
